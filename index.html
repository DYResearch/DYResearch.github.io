What does this technology actually do, and what does its evidence support?

For

Investors screening a deal. Founders preparing for one.

Included

  • An architecture map of the system as built, not as described
  • A claims-to-evidence matrix: every public technical claim traced to code, tests or CI, or marked unverified
  • Engineering health: tests, CI, dependencies, release discipline
  • The risks that matter most, ranked, with the evidence for each
  • Questions to put to the founders

You receive

A one-page executive summary, the written report and the evidence matrix.

We need from you

Access to the repository or product, the deck or technical documentation, and the question you most need answered.

Not included

Focused Audit

Focused Audit

Two to three weeks from confirmed scope

Does this one critical property actually hold?

For

CTOs with a system that has to be right. Investors with one specific technical doubt.

Included — everything in Snapshot, and

  • One property investigated to the bottom: worst-case timing, determinism, concurrency and races, verification claims, or the security of one component
  • The execution model rebuilt from source
  • Worst-case response times with dy-wcet, every iteration shown, where timing is the question
  • The failure chain traced end to end, evidence kept apart from hypothesis
  • Proposed fixes, each naming what would confirm it and what would rule it out
  • A stated confidence boundary: what the evidence proves, and where it stops

You receive

The audit report, the artefacts needed to reproduce it, and one written round of questions after delivery.

The standard

An RP2350 timer that stopped for minutes — a complete analysis, published in full.

We need from you

The above, plus the property in question and any logs, traces or measurements you already hold.

Not included

Instrumentation on a bench, changes to your code, and a second property, which is quoted separately.

Due Diligence

Three to four weeks from confirmed scope

Is the technology what the company says it is — and what could break the investment?

For

Investors before a term sheet. Acquirers. Boards.

Included — everything in Focused Audit, across the whole system, and

Repository access, the deck and the technical materials in the data room, and one technical contact who can answer in writing.

Not included

Legal, financial or commercial diligence, and investment advice. The findings are technical; the decision stays yours.
